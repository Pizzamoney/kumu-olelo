Move: Arrow keys / WASD (or the D-pad) — now with a smooth glide. A button / Space / Enter: talk, check, confirm. B / Esc: notebook. Walk the loops, work the quests, and watch the light change: morning gives way to golden hour, then dusk, then night, and the windows light up as it does. Campfires smoke and spark, leaves drift across the loops, and the shoreline splashes under your sneakers. Campers have faces when they talk now — and in battle, nothing stands still. Critters in the tall woods bite back: battles are turn-based, and your HP rolls down slow, so a bandage used fast can save you. In battle, the enemy's stats stay pinned in the dark strip above your party line — its HP on a live meter, its ATK, the XP it pays, and its attacks — so you can size up a fight before you swing.
