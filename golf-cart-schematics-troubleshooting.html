⚠ Shop safety — read once, every time
  • Run tests with the rear wheels off the ground on jack stands — a cart that suddenly wakes up will move.
  • Switch to TOW (Run/Tow or Tow/Maintenance) before disconnecting any cable on sepex/IQ carts.
  • No rings, watches, or dangling sleeves around battery terminals. A shorted pack will weld a wrench.
  • Batteries vent hydrogen and hold sulfuric acid — no sparks or smoking at the pack, eye protection on.
  • Chock the front wheels and set the brake before you crawl anywhere near it.
Before any of these: put your meter across the main pack + and − and eyeball every cable end. Half of all “dead carts” are a discharged pack or one green, fuzzy cable lug — the wizard starts there on purpose.
