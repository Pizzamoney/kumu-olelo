RECENT

Build the campground people come back to.

You start with 30 days, $10,000, and a rough patch of pine country — but the season no longer has to end at Day 30. Keep running month by month, chase grants with the Pitch Builder mini-game, borrow smart with DSCR loans, and bolt on add-ons like Wi-Fi and kayak rentals. The map has 34 usable pads — Loop A runs A1–A17 and Loop B runs B1–B17, numbered in order around each loop — plus 12 problem sites (C1–C12) that stay unusable until you pay to clear what blocks them: a fallen oak, standing water, stumps, junk. Cleared problem sites become brand-new buildable pads. Start with tents and upgrade them all the way up — tent → RV pad → cabin — and add a picnic or event pavilion to give guests a place to gather. Comfort starts the way it does at every primitive park: with a porta potty — or skip straight to a full bathhouse if the park can afford it. A porta potty can always be upgraded into a bathhouse later, on the same pad. Any site can also be demolished for a salvage refund when you want its pad back for something else. You set the nightly rent yourself in the Build panel: every site type starts at its base rate, and you can raise it for more per night or cut it to fill more pads. Pine Hollow is open year-round now: June–August is peak season, spring and fall are the shoulder seasons, and winter runs quiet — so build camp host sites, hire hosts (couples when you can get them, singles when you can't), keep them happy with fair pay, gentle site rent, and perks, and throw host-run events to pull campers in during the slow months. One host covers about six guest sites before ratings start to slide, and the camp store needs a host on the counter or it stays closed.

DSCR Loans

A DSCR loan is underwritten on the park’s cash flow, not your personal income. DSCR = Monthly Net Operating Income ÷ Monthly Debt Payment. Lenders want 1.20× or higher after the new loan. Borrow, build, and the daily payment comes out automatically.

Hosts & Hiring

Hosts keep the park running: roughly one host per 6 guest sites, plus one on the camp store counter or the store stays closed. Cover too many sites with too few hosts and the rating slides. Pay them fairly, keep their site rent gentle, and reach for a perk before morale — and a host — walks.

Host Events

Pine Hollow is open year-round, and events are how a year-round park fights the slow months. Each event needs enough hosts on staff to pull it off, costs money up front, and boosts occupancy for 3 days.
