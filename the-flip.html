The Flip

It's 11:00 AM Friday. Five sites turned over this morning — or they were supposed to. Check-out was an hour ago, check-in is at 2:00 PM, and you're the dispatcher. Work each site in the real order and get it honestly Ready.

1 · Verify firstA site is occupied until checkout or confirmation — not until it looks empty. Read the pad: an RV gone isn't a site empty when the chairs are still out, the rug's still down, or the cord's still plugged in — and the placard sign tells you if a departure's actually been verified. Skip this and a ghost site will burn you.
2 · Safety before everythingA smoldering fire ring gets doused before trash, before anything. Out of order costs you.
3 · Clean, then testTrash, ring, table, gravel — then pedestal, water, sewer. A clean site with no power isn't Ready. It's a 7 PM complaint.
4 · Handle the findsLost gear, damage, dead pedestals: return it, charge or waive it, fix it or band-aid it — and log the promise.

The park clock is always running. Thorough is safe, rushing is fast — until the arrival finds what you missed in tonight's review.
